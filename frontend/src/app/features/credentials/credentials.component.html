What is this?

Your Tübingen University credentials (starting with zx) are used to authenticate with the ILIAS learning management system.

These credentials are stored encrypted and are only used to fetch your course information from ILIAS on your behalf.

Security

  • Credentials are stored encrypted
  • Transmitted over HTTPS only
  • Never shared with third parties
  • You can update them anytime

💡 Tip

After saving your credentials, head to My Courses and click Refresh from ILIAS to load your enrolled courses.
